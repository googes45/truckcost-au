A straight-up note: TruckingCostr AU is currently a beta, not a production-ready paid service or approved compliance product. The electronic diary is not represented as an NHVR-approved Electronic Work Diary and does not calculate legal fatigue limits. GPS may stop when the app is backgrounded or the device is locked. ATO/BAS reminders are private and user-entered; direct ATO lodgement is not available. Subscription billing and shared public driver feeds are not live. Calculations are estimates based on information entered by the user.
