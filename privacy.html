Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

TruckingCostr AU is a beta truck operating-cost and job-record app. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Account authentication and cloud workspace storage are provided by Supabase, with the production database located in Sydney, Australia.

Information collected and stored

Information you enter may include your email address, truck and registration details, odometer readings, jobs, revenue and costs, fuel purchases, servicing, tyre records, trip routes and GPS points, manual diary entries, weighbridge reports and jokes. Your signed-in workspace is stored in Supabase and a copy may be held in your browser. JSON exports are plain files and may contain sensitive business and location details.

If you grant browser permission and start GPS trip recording, location and speed points may be recorded and synchronised with your workspace. GPS recording may be interrupted by your device or browser. Receipt images selected for manual review are not uploaded or retained as attachments; details you manually enter may be saved.

Lifetime complimentary access

The app stores a limited entitlement record for people the owner selects for lifetime complimentary access. This record includes the invited email address, account association, grant date, status and optional reason. It is used to identify eligible accounts and is not a public list.

Sharing and service providers

Your workspace is private to your signed-in account under database row-level security. The current jokes and weighbridge reports are stored within that private workspace; the shared public driver feed is not yet connected. GitHub Pages, Supabase and the jsDelivr script CDN may process technical information under their own privacy policies. External links such as Google Maps, NHVR and NMI are governed by those providers' terms.

Security, retention and requests

We use Supabase authentication and row-level security, but no online system can guarantee absolute security. Use a unique password, sign out on shared devices and protect exported backups. Account/workspace data and access-grant records are retained until removed through an authorised process. To request access, correction or deletion, contact jeemswelding2@gmail.com. Do not send your password.

Contact and changes

This policy may change as features are added. For privacy questions, contact jeemswelding2@gmail.com.
