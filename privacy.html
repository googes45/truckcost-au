Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

This policy describes the current TruckingCostr AU beta, a web app hosted on GitHub Pages with account authentication and a cloud workspace database provided by Supabase. It is not a statement that every planned social or subscription feature is live.

In short: when you sign in, your workspace is synchronised to a Supabase project hosted in Sydney, Australia. Your records are associated with your account and protected by database row-level security. A local browser copy and downloadable JSON backup may also exist.

Information the app handles

Depending on what you enter, the workspace can include your email address, truck and registration details, odometer readings, jobs, revenue and costs, fuel purchases, servicing, tyre records, trip routes and GPS points, manual diary entries, weighbridge reports and jokes. The app uses Supabase email/password authentication. Supabase processes authentication details and stores the account workspace for the app.

When you start GPS trip recording and grant browser permission, the app records location and speed points. Those trip records are included in your workspace synchronisation when you are signed in. GPS recording can be interrupted by your device or browser.

The receipt workflow lets you select an image for manual review. The image itself is not uploaded or retained by the app; receipt details that you enter are saved in your workspace.

Where information is stored and service providers

The production Supabase database project is configured in Sydney, Australia. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details and access logs. The app loads the Supabase JavaScript client from jsDelivr. These providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

Workspace records are saved in the Supabase database under your account. Row-level security restricts workspace access to the signed-in account owner. A copy is also stored in the current browser profile for app operation. The app does not currently provide fleet/tenant sharing, an account deletion button, or a self-service data export from the cloud; use the in-app JSON backup for a local export and contact us for account/data requests.

Driver posts and weighbridge information

The current app's jokes and driver weighbridge reports are stored inside each user's private workspace. The shared public joke feed and shared live weighbridge status reporting are not yet connected to the app, even though database tables have been prepared for that later feature. Do not assume a post is visible to other drivers.

National weighbridge directory entries may be incomplete or out of date. Confirm access and operating status with the site operator or relevant authority.

Security, backups and retention

Supabase authentication and database row-level security are enabled for the production workspace tables. No system can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password private, use a unique password, sign out on shared devices and protect exported backup files. JSON backups are plain files and may contain sensitive business and location information.

Workspace data is retained in the cloud until it is deleted by an authorised administrative process. To request access, correction or deletion of your account or workspace data, contact jeemswelding2@gmail.com. Do not send your password. We may need to verify that you control the account before acting.

Third-party links and children

Links may open Google Maps, the National Measurement Institute or the National Heavy Vehicle Regulator. Their own privacy practices apply. TruckingCostr AU is a business utility and is not designed for children.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as features are added. For privacy questions or requests, contact jeemswelding2@gmail.com. Do not post private personal, vehicle, financial or GPS information in public project issues.
