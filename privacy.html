Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

TruckingCostr AU is a beta truck operating-cost and job-record app. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Account authentication and cloud workspace storage are provided by Supabase, with the production database located in Sydney, Australia.

Information collected and stored

Information you enter may include your email address, truck and registration details, odometer readings, jobs, revenue and costs, fuel purchases, servicing, tyre records, trip routes and GPS points, manual diary entries, weighbridge reports, jokes and tax due-date reminders you choose to save. Your signed-in workspace is stored in Supabase and a copy may be held in your browser. JSON exports are plain files and may contain sensitive business and location details.

If you grant browser permission and start GPS trip recording, location and speed points may be recorded and synchronised with your workspace. GPS recording may be interrupted by your device or browser. Receipt images selected for manual review are not uploaded or retained as attachments; details you manually enter may be saved.

Tax reminders and government services

Tax and BAS reminders are dates and notes entered by you and stored within your private workspace. This beta does not connect to ATO systems, retrieve your tax records, prepare a verified BAS or lodge tax returns. No tax information is sent to the ATO or NHVR through this reminder feature. Any future ATO integration will require separate authorisation, privacy and security assessment, and clear user consent.

NHVR notices

The planned NHVR notice channel is designed for one-way publication of official notices only. It is not currently connected or endorsed by NHVR. The design does not grant NHVR access to driver or fleet accounts, GPS, trips, jobs, financial records, user posts or other personal information, and does not provide a return-data feed.

Lifetime complimentary access

The app stores a limited entitlement record for people the owner selects for lifetime complimentary access. This record includes the invited email address, account association, grant date, status and optional reason. It is used to identify eligible accounts and is not a public list.

Sharing and service providers

Your workspace is private to your signed-in account under database row-level security. The current jokes and weighbridge reports are stored within that private workspace; the shared public driver feed is not yet connected. GitHub Pages, Supabase and the jsDelivr script CDN may process technical information under their own privacy policies. External links such as Google Maps, NHVR and NMI are governed by those providers' terms.

Security, retention and account deletion

Account deletion instructions

We use Supabase authentication and row-level security, but no online system can guarantee absolute security. Use a unique password, sign out on shared devices and protect exported backups. You can permanently delete your account from More Stuff → Delete account & cloud data inside the app. This deletes your sign-in account, private cloud workspace, linked lifetime-free access grant, jokes, weighbridge reports and subscription entitlement records held by this service. Deletion cannot be undone. Export any records you wish to keep before confirming. Deletion removes the cloud copy and the current device's app data, but cannot remotely erase browser/device copies on your other devices or files you previously exported; clear those local copies and securely delete exported backups yourself. If you cannot access your account, request help at jeemswelding2@gmail.com. Do not send your password.

Contact and changes

This policy may change as features are added. For privacy questions, contact jeemswelding2@gmail.com.
