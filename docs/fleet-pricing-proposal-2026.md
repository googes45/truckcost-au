## Owner-selected device arrangement: bundled under minimum-term contract
TruckingCostr AU supplies compatible devices as part of a **minimum-term fleet contract**. The total customer fee must be quoted as **software fee + device-service component** (including device recovery, connectivity, installation, MDM, support, replacement reserve, finance and margin). Do not describe devices as free. Do not activate a fixed device-inclusive price until supplier quotations, operational testing and margins are approved.

**Indicative 100-truck, 24-month example (assumptions only, not a quote):**
- Devices: 100 × $250 = $25,000
- Mounts/chargers: 100 × $60 = $6,000
- Setup/shipping: 100 × $50 = $5,000
- Upfront hardware/deployment: $36,000; straight-line recovery over 24 months = $1,500/month
- Connectivity: 100 × $15/month = $1,500/month
- Device management/support: 100 × $8/month = $800/month
- **Baseline device cost allowance = $3,800/month**, before finance, breakage, replacement reserve, staff overhead, tax and profit
- **Software price = $3,100/month; baseline combined cost/revenue hurdle = $6,900/month** using this simplified cost allowance and the software sticker price. This is NOT a true break-even or profit calculation: $3,100 includes potential GST, and software delivery, taxes and payment costs are excluded.
- **Illustrative bundled customer price = $7,900/month for 100 trucks, 24-month minimum**, equivalent to $79/truck/month. This is an unapproved modelling example only. If GST applies at 10%, the GST-inclusive $7,900 contains $718.18 GST, leaving $7,181.82 before other costs. Subtracting the $3,800 assumed device allowance leaves $3,381.82 to cover software delivery, other costs and profit; actual device input costs and GST credits require separate treatment.

For other tiers, quote device bundles individually using the actual number of deployed units and supplier costs; do not simply apply the 100-truck device unit economics to 5- or 10-truck fleets. A 36-month option may reduce monthly capital recovery but increases contract and replacement exposure. Set a minimum margin and stress-test early termination, unpaid invoices, lost devices, SIM overages and replacements.

## Commercial safeguards required in every device-inclusive quote
1. Specify device models, count, delivery/activation dates, accessories, data allowances, network coverage and support hours.
2. Define title/ownership, return requirements at contract end, acceptable wear, loss/damage, repair/replacement and insurance.
3. Require a signed 24- or 36-month minimum commitment, reasonable early termination recovery terms and any applicable consumer-law protections; obtain legal review.
4. Consider an upfront deployment fee/deposit where commercially appropriate, with transparent treatment of GST, refunds and equipment return.
5. Include price-adjustment terms for SIM/data and supplier increases, subject to clear notice and applicable law; prevent uncontrolled roaming/overages.
6. Secure supplier quotes and a contingency/replacement reserve; verify the hardware's suitability for real-world trucking conditions.
7. Validate software security, tenant separation, fleet permissions, audit trails and billing entitlements before paid deployment.
8. Keep NHVR approval status explicit. No claim that the manual diary is an approved EWD.

## Implementation and approval gates
- Confirm ABN/GST registration effective date, customer invoicing and platform sales rules.
- Obtain 2–3 supplier quotations for devices, mounting, data, MDM, installation and warranty.
- Run an actual 5–10 truck pilot, including GPS and background behaviour, support load and connectivity.
- Build fleet tenancy, driver roles, invitations, server-verified entitlements and secure payments.
- Validate fully burdened per-tier margins and approve final customer-facing bundled prices.
- Publish customer terms and obtain legal, privacy and security reviews.

**Approved direction:** revised software tiers, including 100 trucks at $3,100/month, and devices supplied only under a minimum-term bundled contract. **Not approved:** a final device-inclusive retail price, live billing, or an NHVR-approved EWD claim.
