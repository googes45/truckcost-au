Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

This policy describes the current TruckingCostr AU public beta, a static web application hosted through GitHub Pages. It does not describe a future cloud or account-based product.

In short: workspace information is stored in local browser storage on your device. There are no user accounts, cloud database, cross-device synchronisation or shared driver posts.

Information stored by the app

Depending on use, the app may store information you enter: an operator email label, truck and registration details, odometer readings, jobs, revenue and costs, fuel purchases, servicing, tyre records, trip and diary entries, weighbridge status reports and jokes. This information is stored in your browser's local storage. The email field is only a local label, not an account or login credential.

GPS trip recording accesses device location after you start a trip and grant browser permission. Location points are saved in the local workspace and are not sent to a TruckingCostr AU server.

The receipt workflow allows an image to be selected for manual review, but does not upload it, run OCR or retain it as an app attachment. Receipt details you enter are saved locally as an expense.

Use and sharing

Locally stored information is used to display records, calculations, reports and exports. The current app does not transmit workspace records, GPS points, jokes or weighbridge reports to a TruckingCostr AU cloud service. Jokes and weighbridge reports are visible only in the same browser workspace and are not shared with other drivers.

GitHub Pages hosts the public website. GitHub may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details and access logs under its own policies. Links may open Google Maps, the National Measurement Institute or the National Heavy Vehicle Regulator. Their own privacy practices apply; TruckingCostr AU does not control those services.

Browser storage, backups and deletion

Local browser storage is not encrypted by this app. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to access the workspace. Clearing browser/site data, device loss or browser failure may remove records. Do not rely on this beta as the only copy of important business information.

You can export a JSON backup from More Stuff. The backup is a plain file and may contain sensitive information; protect it accordingly. You can restore a backup or use Reset data to clear the current workspace. You can also clear site data in browser settings. Deleting local data does not delete technical hosting logs or information held by third parties.

Children

TruckingCostr AU is a business utility and is not designed for children.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as the app develops. For questions or to report a privacy issue, use the project issue tracker. Do not post private personal, vehicle or financial information in a public issue.
