Delete your TruckingCostr AU account

You can permanently delete your account and associated cloud data from inside the app.

  1. Sign in to your TruckingCostr AU account.
  2. Open More Stuff.
  3. Choose Delete account & cloud data.
  4. Read the warning and confirm the deletion.

Deletion removes your sign-in account, private cloud workspace, linked lifetime-free access grant, jokes, weighbridge reports and subscription entitlement records held by this service. The request is processed by the authenticated account-deletion service.

Important: Deletion is permanent. Export any records you want to keep first. It removes the cloud copy and the current device's app data, but cannot remotely erase browser/device copies on your other devices or files you previously exported. Clear those local copies and securely delete exported backups yourself.

Can't access your account?

Email jeemswelding2@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account and ask for account deletion. Do not send your password. We may need to verify account ownership before processing the request.
